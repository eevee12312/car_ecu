# Advanced Requirements & Features for Your R35 GT-R Nismo Python Simulator

### 1. **Engine & Powertrain Dynamics**

* **VR38DETT Engine Model:**

  * Turbocharged V6 with twin turbos.
  * Model boost dynamics: spool-up, wastegate behavior, turbo lag.
  * Realistic torque curve: RPM-dependent torque with boost pressure effects.
  * Variable intake/exhaust effects, intercooler efficiency.
  * Exhaust backpressure effects on performance.
* **Gearbox and Drivetrain:**

  * 6-speed dual-clutch transmission simulation with shifting delays.
  * Simulate clutch slip, torque converter lock-up.
  * AWD torque split (front/rear bias with adjustable parameters).
  * Drivetrain loss modeling (\~10-15%).

### 2. **Vehicle Dynamics & Traction**

* **Suspension:**

  * Simplified but effective suspension model: spring/damper, roll stiffness.
  * Effect of suspension on traction and handling.
* **Tires:**

  * Pacejka tire model or similar for grip curves (lateral and longitudinal).
  * Tire wear and temperature effects on grip.
* **Traction Control & Stability Control:**

  * Model Nissan’s VDC (Vehicle Dynamics Control).
  * Detect wheel slip and adjust engine torque / braking accordingly.
  * Allow enabling/disabling traction control for more challenge.
* **Launch Control:**

  * Realistic launch control with RPM limits, wheel slip detection.
  * Staged launch with torque ramp-up.

### 3. **Environmental Factors**

* **Road Conditions:**

  * Simulate dry, wet, icy surfaces affecting grip.
  * Effects of road slope/incline on acceleration and braking.
* **Weather Effects:**

  * Temperature affecting engine performance (air density).
  * Rain simulation reducing tire grip and traction.

### 4. **Aerodynamics**

* Model drag and downforce based on speed.
* Simulate adjustable rear wing effects on downforce and drag.
* Effect of aero on stability at high speeds.

### 5. **Telemetry & Sensors**

* Real-time data logging for:

  * RPM, torque, horsepower.
  * Boost pressure, throttle position.
  * Wheel speeds (all 4 wheels).
  * Vehicle speed, lateral and longitudinal G-forces.
  * Tire temperatures and pressures.
* Ability to export telemetry data for analysis.

### 6. **Fault Simulation & Wear**

* Simulate engine overheating and thermal limits.
* Turbocharger overheating and degradation.
* Fuel consumption and fuel system simulation.
* Wear and tear: progressive loss of performance if abused.
* Fault codes and warnings (e.g., knock detection, boost leaks).

### 7. **Driver Inputs & Controls**

* Support multiple input devices: joystick/throttle, keyboard, gamepad.
* Realistic pedal and steering input response curves.
* Advanced shifting modes: manual, auto, paddle shifters.
* ABS braking system simulation.
* Rev limiter with realistic cut and engine response.

### 8. **Challenge Modes**

* Time attack with lap timing and sector splits.
* Drag race mode with 0-60 and 1/4 mile timings.
* Drift mode with slip angle and score tracking.
* Realistic tire warm-up and degradation during sessions.

### 9. **User Interface & Feedback**

* Dynamic gauges: tachometer, speedometer, boost, temp, gear indicator.
* Warning lights: check engine, boost overpressure, traction control.
* Audio feedback: engine sounds, turbo flutter, tire squeal.
* HUD with lap times, best times, gear display, current torque/HP.
